CUONG CHIEN ONLINE - HUONG DAN NGUOI MOI (v20)

QUAN TRONG
- Ban game hien tai la ban NGUYEN MAU dang phat trien, chua phai MMORPG hoan chinh de phat hanh.
- Supabase cua du an da duoc cau hinh tren may chu. Khong chay lai cac file SQL trong thu muc database neu khong co huong dan cu the; chay lai co the ghi de logic dang hoat dong.
- Khong dua service_role key vao game, khong gui mat khau tai khoan cho nguoi ho tro.
- Khong co APK san trong goi nay. Can Godot va Android SDK/export templates de tao APK.

NEU KHONG BIET GODOT: CHI CAN LAM 2 VIEC DAU
1. Tren may tinh Windows, tai Godot Standard tu https://godotengine.org/download/windows/ va giai nen.
2. Tai va giai nen file ZIP nay. Mo Godot, bam Import, chon file project.godot trong thu muc CuongChienOnline_Inventory_Project, sau do bam Import & Edit.
3. Bam nut tam giac Run Project. Neu xuat hien loi, chup anh tab Debugger/Errors gui nguoi ho tro.

KIEM TRA DANG NHAP VA CHOI ONLINE
1. Can ket noi Internet.
2. Tao tai khoan thu nghiem bang email hop le va mat khau tu 6 ky tu. Neu Supabase bat xac minh email, mo email xac minh truoc khi dang nhap.
3. Dang nhap, vao map lang. Kiem tra nhan vat, HP/MP, quai, tan cong va tui do.
4. Muon kiem tra multiplayer that, can dang nhap 2 tai khoan tren 2 thiet bi khac nhau. Khong dung chung tai khoan de test dong thoi.
5. Neu loi, gui anh loi va noi ro loi xuat hien luc mo game, dang nhap hay danh quai.

TAO APK ANDROID (SAU KHI GAME CHAY DUOC TREN WINDOWS)
1. Cai OpenJDK 17 va Android Studio tu cac trang chinh thuc.
2. Mo Android Studio lan dau va de no cai Android SDK.
3. Trong Godot: Editor > Editor Settings > Export > Android. Dien Java SDK Path va Android SDK Path theo thu muc da cai.
4. Trong Godot: Editor > Manage Export Templates > Download dung phien ban Godot dang dung.
5. Mo Project > Export, chon Android APK - Test, bam Export Project va luu file build/CuongChienOnline.apk.
6. Cai APK len dien thoai de thu. Chi lam AAB sau khi APK va multiplayer da duoc kiem thu.
